The Book Fund

A quarter of every item's price, not counting shipping, goes toward five HBCU students' 2027 book costs. We'll share who it reaches and how it's spent. Want to give more? Become a founding member of the Rundown and $100 goes straight to the fund.

One of one

Every piece here is a single copy. Once it's in your bag and paid for, it's yours, and it disappears from the shelf.

Shipping

Books and CDs go USPS Media Mail: $4.99 for the first, $1 for each one after. Magazines and anything else go Ground Advantage, priced at checkout.

Packed like it matters

Magazines ship flat in rigid mailers. Books get wrapped. We pack the way we'd want ours packed.

Questions

Condition notes are honest and photos are the actual item. Ask before you buy. All sales final unless it arrives damaged.
